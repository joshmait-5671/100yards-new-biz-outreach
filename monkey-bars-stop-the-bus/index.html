How it plays

You call a letter. They fill the grid. Somebody finishes and yells. Then the real part starts — everybody has to defend their answers.

1

Call a letter

Pick any letter out loud. Everybody writes it at the top of their sheet and the clock starts.

2

Fill the grid

One word per category, all starting with that letter. Noun, verb, adjective, a word with a prefix — your categories, your call.

3

Stop the bus

First one done shouts it. Pencils down. Then you read answers aloud and anything a classmate can challenge gets defended or dropped.
